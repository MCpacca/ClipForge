1. What ClipForge is

ClipForge is a personal desktop application that runs on its owner's computer. It edits long videos into short clips and, when the owner connects their own YouTube channels, uploads and schedules the clips the owner has approved. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.

4. Access and removal

You can disconnect a channel at any time in ClipForge's settings, or remove ClipForge's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting stops all uploads to that channel.
