ClipForge

A personal tool for turning long videos into short clips.

ClipForge is a private desktop application that runs on its owner's own computer. It finds the most interesting moments in long videos, edits them into vertical short clips with captions, and helps plan when to post them.

How it uses YouTube

When its owner connects their own YouTube channel, ClipForge uploads the clips the owner has approved to that channel, adds their title, description and caption track, and schedules when they go public. It only acts on the channels its owner signs in with, and only after the owner approves each upload.

ClipForge is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.
