Privacy Policy

ClipForge · Last updated 28 October 2026

ClipForge is a personal desktop application used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains how it handles data from Google and YouTube.

What ClipForge accesses

When the owner connects a YouTube channel, ClipForge asks Google for permission to:

  • upload videos to that channel and set their title, description, tags, schedule and thumbnail;
  • add caption tracks to those videos;
  • read the channel's name and ID, and the privacy status of videos ClipForge uploaded, to confirm they went public.

How the data is used

This access is used only to upload and schedule the owner's own approved clips to the owner's own channel. ClipForge does not read, collect or analyse other people's data, and does not use Google user data for advertising.

Where the data is kept

The sign-in token Google provides is stored only on the owner's computer. ClipForge has no servers or databases of its own: videos go directly from the owner's computer to YouTube. No Google user data is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ClipForge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. ClipForge also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time by disconnecting the channel in ClipForge's settings, or by removing ClipForge at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access stops all uploads; the stored token is then useless and is deleted from the owner's computer.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the owner through the Clipaholic YouTube channel.
